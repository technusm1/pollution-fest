# Research notes: kharif maize vs paddy in Punjab

Compiled 2026-11-12 by a research agent; ✓ = fetched and figure found on page / in extracted PDF text. UNVERIFIED items must not go on the site until checked.

## Verdict

"Private demand will make kharif maize the farmer's own best choice" **does not hold today**. Agronomy holds (water, timing, stalks); economics fails: paddy out-earns kharif maize by ~₹33,000/acre/year on CACP's own Punjab data, and the only big demand channel (ethanol) is set and then undercut by government policy.

## Net returns, Punjab (CACP KMS 2025-26, TE2023-24 actuals) ✓

[CACP Kharif 2025-26](https://cacp.da.gov.in/Document/EnglishReports/KharifReports2025-26_20250825125215821.pdf), Annex 5.2 (PDF p.240–241), 5.9 (p.312)

| ₹/ha | Cost A2 | Cost A2+FL | Gross value of output | Returns over A2+FL |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Paddy | 54,296 | 60,331 | 152,161 | **91,830** |
| Maize | 47,517 | 59,193 | 68,538 | **9,345** |

- Gap ₹82,485/ha ≈ **₹33,400/acre**. CACP (p.180): "Paddy was the most profitable crop in Punjab, while returns from maize were significantly lower due to low productivity." CACP flags Punjab's maize sample as inadequate (p.181).
- Previous report (TE2022-23): paddy ₹87,030/ha vs maize ₹4,998/ha over A2+FL. ✓ [CACP 2024-25](https://cacp.da.gov.in/Document/EnglishReports/english_20241213134956901.pdf)
- Projected yields KMS 2025-26 (Annex 5.5): paddy 71.10 q/ha, maize 35.16 q/ha. C2: paddy ₹1,593/q, maize ₹2,237/q.
- Illustration (ours): at a PAU hybrid yield of ~60 q/ha and a realised ₹2,400/q, maize gross ≈ ₹144,000/ha, close to paddy's ₹152,161. **The gap is yield (farm 35–43 q/ha vs hybrid 52–62) plus unrealised price.**

## Policy tilt ✓

[ICRIER 2026 executive summary](https://www.icrier.org/pdf/ES_diversification-to-augment-farmers-incomes-and-promote-sustainable-agriculture-in-punjab-and-haryana.pdf): total incentives to paddy in Punjab ₹40,577/ha (2022-23). Punjab crop-diversification budget 2024-25 ₹500 crore → revised ₹40 crore → **actual ₹0.85 crore**. ICRIER proposes ₹35,000/ha/yr for 5 years.

## Yields and the wrong season

- Punjab maize 2024-25: 86.1 thousand ha, 43.03 q/ha; paddy 32.43 lakh ha, 66.70 q/ha. PAU hybrids 21–25 q/acre, 95–99 days; PMH 17 (2025) 25 q/acre, "suitable for ethanol". ✓ [PAU Package of Practices Kharif 2026](https://www.pau.edu/content/ccil/pf/pp_kharif.pdf)
- Farmers 37.18 q/ha vs recommended hybrids 57.48 q/ha; wheat after maize yields 4–5 q/ha more. ✓ [Tribune May 2023](https://tribuneindia.com/news/coordinated-push-needed-to-spur-maize-farming-510109)
- **Spring maize is "another paddy"** (PAU soil chemist), 18–20 irrigations. ✓ [DTE Oct 2024](https://www.downtoearth.org.in/agriculture/spring-maize-crop-aggravating-punjabs-groundwater-crisis). Over 1.46 lakh ha spring/summer maize vs ~90,000 ha kharif; PAU breeder: "even more water-intensive than paddy". ✓ [Tribune Jun 2025](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/punjab/agri-dept-plans-to-impose-curbs-on-spring-maize-in-punjab-629882). Summer maize 105–120 cm water vs kharif 40 cm. ✓ [Tribune Feb 2025](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/punjab/decisive-action-needed-to-manage-maize-cultivation-in-punjab)

## Water ✓

- Kharif maize PAU trial: irrigation 206–225 mm, total use ~535 mm, ~47 q/ha. [IJAS 91(6) 2021](https://epubs.icar.org.in/index.php/IJAgS/article/download/114246/44509/338122)
- Puddled transplanted rice: up to 130.3 cm irrigation. [IJAS 91(9) 2021](https://epubs.icar.org.in/index.php/IJAgS/article/download/116073/45141/337186)
- Maize 800–1,000 L/kg vs rice 3,000–3,500 L/kg (Tribune Feb 2025, quoting PAU).

## Demand: boom then policy-made bust

- 2023–25: an ethanol price premium for maize made India a net corn importer. ✓ [GAIN IN2025-0023](https://agriexchange.apeda.gov.in/MarketReport/999990605202541515452670.pdf)
- ESY 2025-26 allocations ~1,048 crore L (of 1,776 offered); maize 45.68%, FCI rice 22.25%. **Demand is capped by allocation, not by distillery capacity.** ✓ [Chinimandi Oct 2025](https://www.chinimandi.com/esy-2025-26-omcs-allocate-around-1049-crore-litres-for-ethanol-supply-maize-holds-share-of-around-45-per-cent)
- 2025-26: record crop; "higher supplies of government-held rice at subsidized prices for EBP affected initial demand projections for corn"; distillers prefer rice (yield, protein, less aflatoxin). ✓ GAIN IN2026-0019
- **FCI sold rice to ethanol makers ~40% below acquisition cost** (Rajya Sabha reply, July 2026). ✓ [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/india-news/fci-sold-rice-to-ethanol-makers-40-below-acquisition-cost-rs-reply-126072901085_1.html). That rice is largely Punjab paddy: the state pays to grow rice with Punjab's groundwater, then sells it at a loss to undercut the crop that would save the groundwater.
- India now exports corn (~2.5 MMT MY2025/26). ✓ GAIN IN2026-0046

## Timing, moisture, risks

- Sow late May–end June; 95–99 days, so harvest early Sept–early Oct (before paddy, wide window before wheat), but at the tail of the monsoon. PAU portable dryer: 3 t, 25%→15% moisture in 8–10 h. ✓ PAU PoP
- Wet maize crashes in price (Ludhiana, July 2025: ₹2,200 → ₹1,200–1,300 after rain). ✓ [Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/punjab/rain-relief-for-paddy-growers-trouble-for-maize-farmers)
- Fall armyworm in Punjab since 2019 ✓ [Tribune 2021](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/punjab/watch-out-for-fall-armyworm-pau-experts-to-maize-farmers-282315); waterlogging damages young crop (PAU).
- Area trend: 5.67 lakh ha (1973-74, top kharif crop) → 1.65 lakh (2000-01) → 86,100 ha (2024-25) → ~1 lakh ha (2025). Today ≈2.6% of paddy's area.
