# Research notes: private demand that could pull Punjab off paddy

Compiled 2026-11-12 by a research agent; links marked ✓ were fetched and the figure found on the page. UNVERIFIED items must not go on the site until checked.

## Bottom line

- The only private-ish channel large enough to absorb ~1 Mha of Punjab kharif maize (~4.4 Mt at today's 4.39 t/ha, ~6 Mt at PAU-hybrid yields) is **grain ethanol**, and its demand is set by government pricing (OMC prices, blending targets, FCI-rice allocation). Distillers can switch to FCI rice or Bihar/MP maize.
- Feed + starch (Punjab/Haryana industrial demand ~3–4 Mt in 2018) exists but already sources from Bihar/UP; Punjab must win on quality and drying.
- Silage is the most purely market-driven channel and pays well, but it is local and is pulling farmers into water-hungry **spring** maize.
- Straw is a disposal market (₹1.7–1.8/kg vs ~₹3/kg handling), not a profit source.
- No precedent found anywhere of a groundwater-depleting crop shrinking because a better private market appeared.

## Bihar maize (market-driven, but rabi maize replacing wheat, not kharif replacing paddy)

- Area 620.5 → 720.9 thousand ha (2000-01 → 2016-17, CAGR 0.89%); production 1,497 → 3,846 thousand t (CAGR 5.71%): boom came from yield. Rabi yield 7,482 kg/ha vs kharif 2,586 (2016-17). "There is hardly any public procurement of maize… maize… has grown on its own strength in the market dynamics." Gulab Bagh mandi (Purnea) unregulated since 2006 APMC repeal; ~125 feed companies; ~2 Mt traded. ✓ [DES/AERC Bhagalpur 2018](https://desagri.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/2018-19-An-Analysis-of-Supply-Chain-of-Maize-Marketing-and-Possibility-of-its-Value-Addition-in-Bihar-2.pdf)
- All Bihar maize growers buy private hybrid seed; rabi hybrid seed market ~₹250 crore. ✓ [CSISA Note 17, 2020](https://csisa.org/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2020/05/CSISA-Policy-and-Research-Note-17.pdf)
- Downside: traders "decide the price"; distress sales at harvest. ✓ [VillageSquare 2019](https://www.villagesquare.in/?p=6119)

## Buyers in Punjab/Haryana

- Industrial maize demand in Punjab/Haryana 3–4 Mt/yr, ~70% poultry feed; "Punjab produces less than 1 million tonne"; mills buy from Bihar/UP for quality and bulk. ✓ [Tribune 2018](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/archive/business/maize-from-bihar-up-feeds-poultry-of-punjab-haryana-609499)
- Sukhjit Starch: ~600,000 t/yr grind across 4 plants; "mixed model" of extension near plants plus aggregators. ✓ [Earnings call, Jun 2023](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/corporate/SUKHJITS_14062023165931_Transcript.pdf)
- Punjab ethanol capacity 2,260 → 3,860 KLD, which "would need about 37 lakh tons of maize"; PAU hybrids average 24–25 q/acre. ✓ [Tribune Aug 2024](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/ludhiana/sowing-maize-as-paddy-replacement/)
- State average yield 4.39 t/ha (2022-23) vs PAU hybrid potential 6–7 t/ha; warning that "distilleries may relocate or source maize from other states". ✓ [Tribune Feb 2025](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/punjab/decisive-action-needed-to-manage-maize-cultivation-in-punjab)
- Punjab June 2025 pilot: 10,000 ha, ₹17,500/ha, promised procurement at ₹2,400/qtl with no named buyer. ✓ [Babushahi](https://www.babushahi.com/full-news.php?id=205277). MLA pledge to buy at ₹2,400 for ethanol, one season only. ✓ [PTI/The Week, Oct 2025](https://www.theweek.in/wire-updates/national/2025/10/06/des84-pb-maize-mla.html)
- India ethanol ESY 2025-26 to August: maize 345 crore litres, FCI rice 214 crore litres, ~70% of supply from grain. ✓ [Autocar Pro, Sep 2026](https://www.autocarpro.in/news/india-ethanol-supplies-reach-895-crore-litres-by-august-end-134667)
- MSP 2026-27: paddy ₹2,441/qtl, maize ₹2,410 (+₹10 only). ✓ [Rural Voice, May 2026](https://eng.ruralvoice.in/kharif-msp-announced-paddy-support-price-raised-by-rs-72-maize-by-rs-10)
- 2023: spring maize sold at ₹1,400–1,500/qtl; wet maize at ₹700–800. ✓ [Tribune Jul 2023](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/punjab/no-msp-for-maize-farmers-seek-govt-help-523674)

## Silage (truly private, profitable, but local and drives spring maize)

- KVK Bathinda case: silage maker paid ₹24,000/acre (2020) and ₹28,000/acre (2021) for standing green maize; sold bales at ₹5.60/kg; 14,880 bales ≈ ₹93 lakh in 2021. ✓ [Indian Farming, Feb 2024](https://epubs.icar.org.in/index.php/IndFarm/article/download/137163/53913/553831)
- Claim of ₹45,000–50,000/acre for standing crop; bales shipped to Haryana, Rajasthan, Maharashtra, HP, Gujarat. ✓ [Hans India/101Reporters, Jan 2024](https://www.thehansindia.com/news/national/makki-ka-achar-is-the-new-favourite-of-cattle-rearers-in-punjab-850246)
- Spring maize needs 18–20 irrigations, "another paddy" for groundwater (PAU soil chemist). ✓ [Down To Earth, Oct 2024](https://www.downtoearth.org.in/agriculture/spring-maize-crop-aggravating-punjabs-groundwater-crisis). Spring maize area 44,000 ha → ~1 lakh ha (2023). ✓ Tribune Jul 2023 (above)

## Contract farming

- PepsiCo potato: ₹1,500/qtl vs ~₹790/qtl cost; field staff every 15–20 days; on-time payment; quality rejections push small farmers out; Punjab Contract Farming Act 2013 repealed. ✓ [Down To Earth, Jun 2022](https://www.downtoearth.org.in/agriculture/contract-farming-is-yet-to-make-a-mark-in-punjab-here-is-why-83454)
- Verbal contracts, bribery allegations around quality checks. ✓ [Newslaundry 2020](https://www.newslaundry.com/2020/10/14/lessons-from-punjab-will-contract-farming-benefit-or-harm-indian-farmers)

## Straw

- Biomass plants paid ₹169/qtl (2024, unchanged from 2023); a plant executive puts the real cost at ₹300. ✓ [Tribune Sep 2024](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/punjab/no-hike-in-paddy-stubble-price-by-biomass-plants)
- Bales ₹170–180/qtl; Punjab produces 180–200 lakh t of straw, ~30 lakh t used ex-situ. ✓ [PTI/ThePrint, Oct 2023](https://theprint.in/india/punjab-farmers-strike-gold-selling-stubble-bales/1823403)
- Verbio Sangrur: collection costs ₹3,000/t; 15–20 day collection window; a registered farmer burned when the rakers never came. ✓ [Mongabay, Mar 2023](https://india.mongabay.com/2023/03/organic-agriculture-is-critical-for-bio-cng-growth/)

## Cotton

- Private buying far above MSP (₹10,251 vs ₹6,225 MSP at Muktsar, 2022), yet area fell to 2.47 lakh ha, the lowest since 2010. ✓ [Tribune Sep 2022](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/punjab/at-over-rs-10-000-per-quintal-fresh-cotton-price-way-above-msp-in-muktsar-district-427263). Good prices alone didn't stop the decline (pests and risk, per other sources).

## International

- Bangladesh: maize area grew from a few thousand ha (1980s) to >200,000 ha (2007-08) "responding to demand from expanding poultry feed markets"; ~850 L water/kg maize vs >3,000 L/kg boro rice. But it replaced mostly wheat, chili, mustard and vegetables, and boro rice only "in some areas". ✓ [CIMMYT 2008](https://ageconsearch.umn.edu/record/56106/files/bangladesh.pdf)

## What would make buyers sign multi-year contracts (agent synthesis)

Drying and storage at aggregation points; stable multi-year OMC pricing and grain allocation; aggregated volume via FPOs or company extension; enforceable written contracts with fair grading; a landed price at or below Bihar/MP plus freight; higher Punjab yields.
