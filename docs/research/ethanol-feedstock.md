## What a distillery can pay for maize (ESTIMATE, not sourced)

380 L/t × ₹71.86 = ₹27,307/t of revenue + DDGS 0.3 t × ₹13–20k (maize DDGS ~₹13/kg ✓ [GEMA via BioEnergy Times](https://bioenergytimes.com/india-witnesses-rise-in-exports-of-ethanol-byproduct-ddgs-but-price-decline-poses-challenge-for-industry/)) − processing ₹10–15/L (UNVERIFIED; GEMA says fixed costs ~₹9.50/L ✓ [ChiniMandi](https://www.chinimandi.com/single-rate-for-all-grain-based-ethanol-grain-ethanol-manufacturers-association-pitches-for-pricing-reform/)) ≈ **₹2,550–2,950/q** before capital, freight and margin; ~₹114/q less at ₹68.86. Consistent with AIDA (Oct 2026): maize at ₹24/kg worked at ₹71; ₹27–29/kg is uneconomical at ₹68.86.

## Who else has proposed it (so don't claim novelty)

- ICRIER PB77 (Gulati & Adhikary, 2026): "FCI rice should remain a residual feedstock"; ~₹44/kg economic cost vs ₹23.2/kg charged, so 5 MMT ≈ ₹10,000 crore hidden loss. Also cautious on maize (43.8% of maize area irrigated). ✓
- Food Secretary (Mar 2026): "From next year, the government will stop supplying whole-grain rice from FCI stocks to distilleries"; broken rice (~90 lakh t/yr) would replace it. ✓ [ETV Bharat](https://www.etvbharat.com/en/business/govt-plans-to-redirect-90-lakh-tonne-fci-broken-rice-to-ethanol-industry-from-next-year-food-sec-enn26032402851)
- Shiromani Punjab Forum (Oct 2026): link feedstock prices to water use; "Punjab kharif maize compact" with assured procurement and mandi dryers. ✓ [ChiniMandi](https://www.chinimandi.com/ethanol-policy-change-may-hit-punjab-farmers-cooperative-mills-and-groundwater-shiromani-punjab-forum/)
- BKU (Mann) letter to the CM (Aug 2026): "concessional FCI rice should not be allowed to undercut maize"; asks for a 5-year feedstock policy. ✓ [Punjab Outlook](https://punjaboutlook.com/punjab-cannot-seek-crop-diversification-while-weakening-markets-for-maize-sugarcane-and-paddy-straw-mann/)
- Industry is split: AIDA wants more FCI rice; GEMA says cheap rice undercuts maize.
