## Common design flaws (agent synthesis)

1. Every scheme pushes against paddy's built-in advantages (assured MSP purchase, free power, credit and mandi network) with one-off cash or unbacked MSP promises.
2. Opt-in with nothing at stake, while the paddy default stays: uptake 5–25% of targets.
3. Money went to capital (machines), not to running costs or a price for straw.
4. Success measured by outputs (machines, acres registered), not outcomes (burning avoided).
5. Late payments destroyed trust.
6. Centre and state argued over who pays.
7. Ex-situ capacity ~1 Mt vs ~19 Mt of straw.
8. Enforcement is symbolic and shifts burning to unwatched hours.
