# Research notes: groundwater, history and the 2009 Act

Compiled 2026-11-12 by a research agent; ✓ = fetched, figure on page.

## Groundwater

- **CGWB 2024 Punjab factsheet** ✓ ([cgwb.gov.in](https://cgwb.gov.in/cgwbpnm/public/uploads/documents/1742295732461871757file.pdf)): stage of extraction 156.87%; 115 of 153 units over-exploited. Series: 165.77% (2017), 164.42% (2020), 165.99% (2022), 163.76% (2023), 156.87% (2024). CGWB itself: "State may review their free/subsidized electricity policy to farmers…"
- **CGWB 2025 national compilation** ✓ (draft copy on [OpenCity](https://data.opencity.in/dataset/national-compilation-of-dynamic-ground-water-resources-of-india-2025); find the official cgwb.gov.in copy before linking): 156.36%; 111 of 153 over-exploited; 24.89 of 26.27 bcm extracted goes to irrigation (~94.7%). Punjab normal rainfall 565.5 mm.
- **Long-term** ✓ ([CGWB/WRD 2022](https://cgwb.gov.in/old_website/GW-Assessment/GWR-2022-Reports%20State/Punjab.pdf)): over-exploited blocks 53 (1984) → 117 (2020) → 114 (2022). Water table fell more than 15 m in ~39% of the state, 1984–2021; Lehragaga (Sangrur) fell 41.04 m. Rainfall ~1,000 mm in the northeast to <300 mm in the southwest.
- **NASA GRACE** ✓ ([Rodell et al., Nature 2009](https://www.nature.com/articles/nature08238)): 4.0 ± 1.0 cm/yr loss over Rajasthan, Punjab and Haryana; 109 km³ lost, 2002–08.
- **"Exhausted by 2039"**: only from a 2019 draft CGWB report as reported by the [Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/archive/punjab/punjab-well-on-way-towards-being-a-desert-state-in-25-yrs-772592) ("till the depth of 300 metres… will end in 20-25 years"). Not an official CGWB finding; attribute it that way.
- **Rice procurement accounts for "at least 50%" of Punjab's groundwater-table decline over 34 years.** ✓ [Chatterjee, Lamba & Zaveri, Nature Communications 2024](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-024-52858-6)

## Water per kg

- "One kg of rice inherently needs about 5000 litres of water for irrigation in a region like Punjab-Haryana"; 180 cm applied; "serious misalignment in rice cropping patterns with respect to the water resource availability"; "almost free electricity policy… indiscriminate groundwater exploitation". ✓ [ICRIER–NABARD 2018](https://www.icrier.org/pdf/Water_Productivity_Mapping_of_Major_Indian_Crops.pdf)
- Rice "160 cm of water" vs 40 cm for wheat or kharif maize; 20–25 irrigations vs 4–5; FCI procured 87% of Punjab+Haryana rice in KMS 2023-24; rice received ₹38,973/ha in input subsidies (2023-24); ~2/3 of farm power is used June–September. ✓ [ICRIER PB-21, 2024](https://icrier.org/pdf/PB-21.pdf) (icrier.org rejects curl's default User-Agent; use a browser UA)

## History

- Rice area 2.27 lakh ha (1960-61) → 31.49 lakh ha (2020-21) "due to its assured procurement at MSP"; in 1974 rice became the top kharif crop (5.69 lakh ha) ahead of cotton (5.47) and maize (5.22); kharif pulses peaked at 54,900 ha moong (1994-95), now <3,000 ha. ✓ [Dhillon & Kumar, Tribune 2022](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/features/acreage-under-pulses-oilseeds-needs-a-boost-407292)
- Paddy 7% of cropped area (1970-71) → 40% (2021-22). ✓ ICRIER PB-21
- 2025: record 32.46 lakh ha. ✓ ThePrint Dec 2025
- **Free power:** Bhattal (Congress) Jan 1997; Badal (SAD) extended it to all farmers; Amarinder (Congress) withdrew it in 2002 (₹60/hp); restored in 2005. ~₹10,000 crore/yr for agriculture. ✓ [Tribune explainer 2025](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/explainers/explainer-why-power-sector-subsidy-is-eating-into-punjabs-coffers), ✓ [Tribune 2002](https://www.tribuneindia.com/2002/20021017/main4.htm)

## The 2009 Act and burning

- Punjab Preservation of Subsoil Water Act 2009: no nursery before 10 May; transplanting date by notification; penalty ₹10,000/ha/month. ✓ [Gazette via FAOLEX](https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/ind202088.pdf)
- "Groundwater conservation policies appear to be exacerbating the crisis by concentrating agricultural burning in the late fall with a 39% higher peak fire intensity." ✓ [Balwinder-Singh et al., Nature Sustainability 2019](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41893-019-0304-4); peak moved from 24 Oct to 4 Nov ✓ [Cornell](https://news.cornell.edu/stories/2019/07/groundwater-policies-fire-air-pollution-northwest-india)
- After 2010, peak burning shifted ~10–12 days into November; fires +21%; NCR PM2.5 +23–26%. ✓ [Kant et al. (IIRS-ISRO), Sci Rep 2022](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-022-06043-8)
- nature.com 303-redirects bots to a login page; it loads fine in a browser.

## Committees

- Johl 1986: shift at least 20% of paddy+wheat area (secondary source only). Johl 2002: divert 1 Mha each from wheat and paddy, ₹12,500/ha compensation. ✓ [Tribune 2002](https://www.tribuneindia.com/2002/20020710/main8.htm)
- Ahluwalia panel 2020: cut common paddy by ~1 Mha of 3.1 Mha over 6–7 years. ✓ [ThePrint 2021](https://theprint.in/india/governance/even-montek-panel-wanted-to-reform-punjab-farming-like-modi-govt-but-amarinder-got-cold-feet/598266)
